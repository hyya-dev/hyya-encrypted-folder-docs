# FAQ

**→ [Full details at hyya.com/hyya-encrypted-folder.html](https://hyya.com/hyya-encrypted-folder.html)**

## Privacy and encryption

**Is it real encryption, or a hidden folder?**
Real encryption: AES-256. Every file is encrypted the moment it goes into a folder, and the file names
inside are encrypted too. Without unlocking, a folder is unreadable to anyone, including us.

**What can iCloud or Google Drive see?**
A folder's name, how many files it holds and their sizes and dates. Never what is inside, and never
the names of the files.

**Do you have an account or a server?**
No hYYa account, no hYYa server and no analytics of our own. Nothing of yours ever passes through us.
Google Drive sync, if you turn it on, goes straight between your devices and your own Google Drive.

**Does it need the internet?**
Only to sync. Locking, unlocking and adding files all work offline.

**Can someone record my screen while a folder is open?**
- **Mac:** while a folder is open, hYYa's window is left out of screen recordings, screenshots and
  shared screens.
- **Android:** while a folder is open, the app can't be captured in screenshots or recordings, and
  Recent apps shows no preview of it.
- **iPhone and iPad:** the app switcher never shows an open folder.

## Opening folders

**What opens a folder?**
One check: Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint, or your device passcode or password. There is no
separate password on each folder.

**I forgot my passphrase. What now?**
Your recovery key opens all your folders. Make one in **Settings** and keep it somewhere secure, away
from the device. hYYa keeps no copy of this key and can't reset it.

**I lost both the passphrase and the recovery key.**
A device that already opens your folders keeps opening them; make a new recovery key there in
Settings. A new device can't open a folder from another device without one of them. There is no hYYa
account and no password reset, not even through us.

**I deleted a folder or a file by mistake.**
Open **Recently Deleted**: deleted files and folders can be recovered for 30 days. A folder kept in
Google Drive also goes to Google Drive's own trash for 30 days.

## Sync

**How do I open my folders on another device?**
- **iCloud** (iPhone, iPad and Mac): sign in to the same Apple Account; your iCloud folders appear by
  themselves.
- **Google Drive** (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android): in **Settings → Where folders live** (on the Mac,
  the **Folders** tab), tap **Connect** next to Google Drive and sign in with the same Google account.
  Then enter your sync passphrase, or your recovery key.

**Can I sync between a Mac and an Android phone?**
Yes, through your own Google Drive. iCloud folders stay on Apple devices.

**A folder from another device doesn't show up.**
- Check you are signed in to the same Google account (or Apple Account for iCloud) on both devices.
- Pull down on the folder list (on the Mac, click the refresh button beside Folders) to check for changes.
- A folder marked **In another Google account** was made under a different Google account. Connect
  that account to open it.

**Can I add the Google Drive folder that Google Drive for desktop shows on my Mac?**
No. Google Drive for desktop keeps its own copy, which hYYa can't use. Connect Google Drive in hYYa's
Settings instead, and your folders appear there.

## Pro and purchases

**What's free and what's Pro?**
Free: two encrypted folders, kept on this device, and every folder you already have always opens.
Pro, a one-time purchase: new folders in iCloud or Google Drive (Google Drive only on Android),
unlimited folders, Auto-Lock, and unlimited Folder Styler on the Mac. You can try Pro free for 7 days;
nothing is charged automatically.

**I deleted a folder. Why didn't I get a free slot back?**
The free plan counts folders you have made, not folders you have now. Deleting one doesn't free up a
slot.

**I bought Pro on my iPhone. Does it work on my Mac or Android?**
On your Mac and iPad, yes: one App Store purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Google Play and the
App Store are separate stores, so on Android Pro is a separate purchase.

**I reinstalled the app and Pro is gone.**
Tap **Restore Purchases** on the Pro screen (on the Mac: **Settings → Plan → Restore purchase**), signed in with the same Apple Account or Google
account you bought it with.

## Troubleshooting

**The app asks for my passcode instead of Face ID or Touch ID.**
hYYa uses what your device offers. If Face ID, Touch ID or a fingerprint isn't set up, or fails a few
times, the device's own passcode or password opens it.

**Which Mac do I need?**
A Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 26 or later.

**Something else is wrong.**
[Open an issue](https://github.com/hyya-dev/hyya-encrypted-folder-docs/issues) with your device, system
and app version, or write to us at [hyya.com/contact.html](https://hyya.com/contact.html).
